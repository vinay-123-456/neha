# 👑 Neha & Jay — Royal Wedding Invitation

> **Interactive Rajputana Wedding Invitation Microsite**  
> *Jay Singh (Son of Shri Jitendra Singh Vaghela) & Neha Kunwar (Daughter of Late Shri Laxman Singh Chouhan)*  
> Destination: Kothar, Bali, Pali, Rajasthan · Date: Friday, 29th January 2027

---

## ✨ Features & Guest Experience

1. **3D Wine-Velvet Book Opening:**  
   Interactive unboxing with 3D cover flip, gold Ganesh medallion, rose petal shower, and background royal shehnai/sitar soundtrack.
2. **Royal Hero Screen (100svh):**  
   Mobile-first centered screen with couple names, family lineage, and auspicious Vedic invocation.
3. **Save the Date — Pull to Reveal Scroll:**  
   Features a golden rod with a hanging royal silk tassel. Pulling or tapping the tassel unrolls the parchment to reveal the 29th January 2027 date, live real-time countdown clock, and **Add to Google Calendar** button.
4. **Bespoke Themed Festivities Cards:**  
   Dynamic interactive cards with distinct styling:
   - 💛 **Haldi:** Golden yellow theme with marigold garlands and turmeric styling (*Wear Yellow* swatch)
   - 💚 **Mehendi:** Lush mint & emerald green theme with intricate henna motifs (*Wear Green* swatch)
   - 💜 **Royal Sangeet:** Midnight purple & magenta cocktail party lights (*Indo-Western · Purple & Pink* swatch)
   - ❤️ **Baraat & Pheras:** Sacred Vedic temple crimson with holy havan fire aura (*Festive · Red & Gold* swatch)
   - 🪔 **Mata Pujan:** Sacred sandalwood & beige temple theme (*Traditional · Beige* swatch)
   - 🍷 **Grand Reception:** Luxury velvet wine & champagne starlight (*Royal Formal · Wine & Gold* swatch)
5. **How to Reach & Location Card:**  
   Destination card for Post Kothar, Tehsil Bali, Jila Pali with direct **Get Directions** (Google Maps) and **Copy Address** actions.
6. **Family Sign-off & WhatsApp Share:**  
   *"With love, the Vaghela & Chouhan families"* and one-click **Share on WhatsApp** button.

## 🚀 Running Locally

### Development Server:
```powershell
npm.cmd run dev -- --host
```
Open [http://localhost:5173/](http://localhost:5173/) or open on your mobile over Wi-Fi.

### Production Build (Apache / XAMPP / Netlify / Vercel):
```powershell
npm.cmd run build
```
Built output is generated in `dist/`. Through XAMPP Apache, open [http://localhost/wedding/](http://localhost/wedding/).
